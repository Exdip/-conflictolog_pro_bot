CKM Quiz Pro 0.3.23.252-dev.284-STORY-EVIDENCE-ID-REPAIR-FIX

Fixes story-round AI review evidence when AI Puffer returns a real speaker quote with a numeric messageId that points to a different message by the same speaker.

Safety rules:
- missing/unknown IDs may be grounded only from a unique real speaker quote;
- a known speaker ID with a mismatched quote may be repaired only to the unique real speaker message containing that quote;
- IDs belonging to another participant are never remapped;
- fabricated or ambiguous quotes remain rejected.
