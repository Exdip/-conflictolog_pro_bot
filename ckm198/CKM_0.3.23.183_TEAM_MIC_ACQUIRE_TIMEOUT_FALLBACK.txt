0.3.23.183-dev.215-TEAM-MIC-ACQUIRE-TIMEOUT-FALLBACK

Participant/team dictation microphone acquisition hardening.

- Adds a 7-second deadline around the first getUserMedia() request.
- A stream returned after timeout/stale session is immediately stopped and cannot revive dictation.
- On first timeout, enumerates audio inputs and retries once using a concrete deviceId when available.
- If no concrete device is available, retries with relaxed audio-processing constraints.
- A second timeout becomes a visible diagnostic instead of leaving UI at “Разрешите доступ к микрофону…”.
- Keeps 182 STT handshake diagnostics, 181 STT session guard and 180 AI -> manual takeover guard unchanged.
